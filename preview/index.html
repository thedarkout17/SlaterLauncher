REDLINE LAUNCHER

Preview · 2000 × 1200 · same layout as the APK
Tap

Tiles and sidebar icons open their app. The play, skip and seek bar control the demo song.

Long-press an icon

Choose which app it opens. The real launcher remembers your pick.

Long-press the background

Wallpaper, music access and switching back to your original home screen.

Swipe left

Slides in the apps page, like CarPlay. Swipe sideways between pages, and swipe right on the first page to go home. The four-dot button opens it too.

This runs in your browser, so app launches are simulated with a message. The song and app names are examples. On the head unit, the top and bottom bars come from the system and sit over the same wallpaper.
